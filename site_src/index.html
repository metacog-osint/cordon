Where governments and aviation and maritime authorities are warning people away.

US travel advisories and embassy departure status, the UK's and Canada's travel advice, European aviation conflict-zone bulletins, US navigational warnings to mariners, and the FAA's ground stops, ground delay programs, airport closures and temporary flight restrictions, collected every hour from the issuing authorities.

US embassy departure status

Read from each advisory's text (State publishes no field for it); every change is reviewed by a person.

EASA conflict-zone bulletins in force

Sorted by the date each expires unless EASA renews it.

Warnings to mariners in force

NGA broadcast warnings, grouped by what they warn of. The map shows each one where its text places it.

US airport ground stops, delay programs and closures

As the FAA's National Airspace System status lists them, with the reason in the FAA's own words. Cordon adds no cause. A ground stop is marked unusual when more are in effect at once than Cordon's own record of them makes normal.

US temporary flight restrictions

FAA TFRs with an area: space launches and recoveries, security, VIP movements, fires and other hazards, drones over crowds, air shows. Outlines and times as the FAA publishes them (times are UTC in its data). A launch, security or special TFR issued in the last 72 hours is marked new. Standing nationwide notices with no area are not listed.

UK foreign travel advice

Where the UK Foreign, Commonwealth & Development Office advises against all or all but essential travel, and any country whose advice it updated in the last 72 hours, with its own note of what changed.

Canada's travel advisories

Where the Government of Canada advises avoiding non-essential travel or all travel, any change to a level or to regional advisories, and any place it updated in the last 72 hours (editorial changes and updates to embassy contacts aside), with its own note of what changed. The feed also carries every place where Canada advises a high degree of caution or has regional advisories.
