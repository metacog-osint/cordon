UK foreign travel advice

Where the UK Foreign, Commonwealth & Development Office advises against all or all but essential travel, and any country whose advice it updated in the last 72 hours, with its own note of what changed.

Canada's travel advisories

Where the Government of Canada advises avoiding non-essential travel or all travel, any change to a level or to regional advisories, and any place it updated in the last 72 hours (editorial changes and updates to embassy contacts aside), with its own note of what changed. The feed also carries every place where Canada advises a high degree of caution or has regional advisories.

New Zealand's travel advice

Where New Zealand's Ministry of Foreign Affairs and Trade advises avoiding non-essential travel or not travelling, any change to a level or to its note of other levels in some areas, and any destination it updated in the last 72 hours (it gives the day, not the time). The feed also carries every destination where it advises increased caution or where other levels apply in some areas.

Japan's overseas safety information

Where Japan's Ministry of Foreign Affairs advises avoiding non-essential travel, not travelling or evacuating (its danger levels 3 and 4), any change to its levels, and what its embassies and consulates have warned of in the last 72 hours. The ministry publishes in Japanese: Cordon keeps its headline as published and adds an English title from a fixed glossary of its info types and levels, not a translation. The feed also carries every country at level 2.

The Netherlands' travel advice

Where the Dutch Ministry of Foreign Affairs gives an orange (travel only if essential) or red (do not travel) colour code, any change to a colour, and any destination whose page it edited in the last 72 hours. Where the colour differs by area the highest is shown and the destination is marked. The ministry's text is in Dutch; the English words are a fixed glossary.

Travel health notices (CDC)

Every travel health notice CDC has in force, at its own level (1 Practice Usual Precautions, 2 Practice Enhanced Precautions, 3 Reconsider Nonessential Travel, 4 Avoid All Travel), with the places it names. A notice Cordon saw appear or change level in the last 30 days is marked.

Sorted by the date each expires unless EASA renews it.

US airport ground stops, delay programs and closures

As the FAA's National Airspace System status lists them, with the reason in the FAA's own words. Cordon adds no cause. A ground stop is marked unusual when more are in effect at once than Cordon's own record of them makes normal.

FAA prohibitions and advisories for foreign airspace

Where the FAA prohibits US operators from flying (Special Federal Aviation Regulations and prohibition NOTAMs) or warns them of potentially hazardous airspace (advisory NOTAMs), from its Prohibitions, Restrictions and Notices page. It applies to US operators and US-registered aircraft. A section where Cordon saw a notice listed or withdrawn in the last 30 days is marked. The FAA gives no dates; a NOTAM number carries its year (A0054/26 is 2026).

US temporary flight restrictions

FAA TFRs with an area: security, VIP movements, fires and other hazards, drones over crowds, air shows. Outlines and times as the FAA publishes them (times are UTC in its data). Impact is Cordon's measure of what a TFR restricts: high near one of the FAA's 30 busiest airports, over 2,500 km² or reaching FL180 over a wide area; low when small, low and away from them. A security or special TFR (or a VIP TFR of high impact) issued in the last 72 hours is marked new unless its impact is low or it re-issues one held before. Standing nationwide notices with no area are not listed. Space operations TFRs (launches and recoveries) are on Launch.

Warnings to mariners in force

NGA broadcast warnings, grouped by what they warn of. The map shows each one where its text places it.
